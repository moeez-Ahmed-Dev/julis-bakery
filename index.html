Our Story

Baking the Way it Was Meant to Be

At Juli’s Bakery, every morning begins before dawn with the comforting aroma of browned butter, warm vanilla, and toasted yeast. Founded by Julie in Doha, Qatar, our kitchen is dedicated to simple, honest baking techniques passed down through generations.

We don't use shortcuts or pre-mixed doughs. Every single item—from our sweet, sticky Argentinian medialunas to our golden layered croissants—is rolled, shaped, and baked in small batches right here at Snan Hall and featured regularly at Torba Market.
